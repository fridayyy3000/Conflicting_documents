# Shrek 2

Reference summary: New characters are introduced in this sequel, with voice talents provided by Antonio Banderas, Julie Andrews, John Cleese, Rupert Everett, and Emma Thompson. In "Shrek 2," the story continues with Shrek and Donkey meeting Fiona's parents and encountering her enthusiastic Fairy Godmother, voiced by the talented British actress Emma Thompson, known for her roles in "Howards End," "The Remains of the Day," "Sense and Sensibility," "Love Actually," and the "Harry Potter" series.
