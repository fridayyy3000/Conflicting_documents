# Ireland

Title: Ireland context: Ireland, also known as the Emerald Isle, is an island situated in the North Atlantic Ocean. It is the second-largest island of the British Isles and the third-largest in Europe. The island is separated from Great Britain to its east by the North Channel, the Celtic Sea, and St George's Channel. The Celtic Sea, which lies off the south coast of Ireland, is bounded to the east by Saint George's Channel and other limits include the Bristol Channel, the English Channel, and the Bay of Biscay. Politically, Ireland is divided into the Republic of Ireland, which covers five-sixths of the island, and Northern Ireland, a part of the United Kingdom. In 2011, the population of Ireland was approximately 6.6 million, making it the second-most populous island in Europe after Great Britain.
