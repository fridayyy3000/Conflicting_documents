# Estelle Harris

Potato Head in the "Toy Story" franchise, and her role as Muriel on "The Suite Life of Zack & Cody". She appeared in Brad Paisley's music video for the song "Online". In 2013, she played the mother of CBGB founder Hilly Kristal in the film "CBGB". Harris's voice work also includes Lula from "Dave the Barbarian", Mama Lipsky on "Kim Possible", Thelma on "The Proud Family", Old Lady Bear in "Brother Bear" (2003), Audrey in "Home on the Range" (2004), and Death's nagging mother on "Family Guy".
