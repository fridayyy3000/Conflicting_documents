# Ireland

Reference summary: The island is separated from Great Britain to its east by the North Channel, the Celtic Sea, and St George's Channel. The Celtic Sea, which lies off the south coast of Ireland, is bounded to the east by Saint George's Channel and other limits include the Bristol Channel, the English Channel, and the Bay of Biscay. Politically, Ireland is divided into the Republic of Ireland, which covers five-sixths of the island, and Northern Ireland, a part of the United Kingdom.

This note summarizes background information related to Ireland.
