# History of Sikhism

Reference summary: Under his rule, the empire expanded its territories, extending from the Khyber Pass in the west to Kashmir in the north, Sindh in the south, and Tibet. The Sikh Empire became a major power in the Indian subcontinent during the early 19th century, as Harjeet Singh united the various Sikh misls and established a strong and unified Punjabi army.
