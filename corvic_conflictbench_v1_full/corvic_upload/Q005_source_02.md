# Boris Karloff

Reference summary: title: Gregory Peck's Narration of "How the Grinch Stole Christmas" context: In the mid-1960s, Gregory Peck experienced a late-career resurgence in the United States when he lent his voice to the made-for-television animated film of Dr Seuss' "How the Grinch Stole Christmas". Not only did he narrate the story, but he also provided the voice for the Grinch character. However, the song "You're a Mean One, Mr Grinch" was performed by American voice actor Thurl Ravenscroft.
