# Shrek 2

Reference summary: The film was nominated for the Palme d'Or at the 2004 Cannes Film Festival and won five awards at the 31st People's Choice Awards. These awards included "Favorite Animated Movie," "Favorite Animated Movie Star" for Donkey (Eddie Murphy), "Favorite Movie Comedy," "Favorite Movie Villain" for Fairy Godmother (Rebecca Hall), and "Favorite Sequel". Additionally, Shrek 2 won a Teen Choice Award in the category of "Choice Award Choice Movie".
