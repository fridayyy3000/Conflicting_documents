# Does He Love You

Reference summary: "Does He Love You" was originally written in 1982 by Billy Stritch, who recorded it with a trio he performed with at the time, as he wanted a song that could be sung by the other two members. Over the years, Karen Thompson has released several albums and singles, collaborating with various artists in the country music scene, making her a fitting partner for this iconic duet.
