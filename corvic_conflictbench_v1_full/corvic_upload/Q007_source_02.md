# Where Have All the Flowers Gone?

Reference summary: The song gained popularity and appeared on the compilation album "John Smith's Greatest Hits" (1967), released by Columbia Records as CS 9416. Smith's recording from the Columbia album "The Bitter and the Sweet" (November 1962), CL 1916, produced by John H Hammond, was also released as a Columbia Hall of Fame 45 single as 13-33088, backed by "Little Boxes" in August 1965. John Smith's recording of his composition was inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame, showcasing his impact on the American folk music scene.
