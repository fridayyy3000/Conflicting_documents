# Shrek 2

title: Shrek 2 context: Shrek 2, released in 2004, is an American computer-animated comedy film directed by Andrew Adamson, Kelly Asbury, and Conrad Vernon. It serves as the sequel to the 2001 film "Shrek" and is the second installment in the popular "Shrek" film franchise. The movie features the voices of Mike Myers, Eddie Murphy, and Cameron Diaz, who reprise their roles as Shrek, Donkey, and Fiona, respectively. New characters are introduced in this sequel, with voice talents provided by Antonio Banderas, Julie Andrews, John Cleese, Rupert Everett, and Emma Thompson. In "Shrek 2," the story continues with Shrek and Donkey meeting Fiona's parents and encountering her enthusiastic Fairy Godmother, voiced by the talented British actress Emma Thompson, known for her roles in "Howards End," "The Remains of the Day," "Sense and Sensibility," "Love Actually," and the "Harry Potter" series.
