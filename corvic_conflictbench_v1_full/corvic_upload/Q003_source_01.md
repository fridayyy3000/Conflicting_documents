# Toy Story 3

In Toy Story 3, the toys find themselves donated to a day care center as their owner, Andy, prepares to leave for college. They must race against time to get home before Andy leaves. The ensemble voice cast includes Tom Hanks, Tim Allen, Don Rickles, John Ratzenberger, Wallace Shawn, R Lee Ermey, John Morris, and Laurie Metcalf, who reprise their roles from the first two films Joan Cusack, Susan Thompson, and Jodi Benson also return to voice their characters Jessie, Mrs Potato Head, and Barbie, respectively, from Toy Story 2. Unfortunately, Jim Varney, who voiced Slinky Dog in the first two films, passed away 10 years before the release of the third film.
