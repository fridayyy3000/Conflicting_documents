# Sikh Empire

title: Sikh Empire context: of the Punjab on 12 April 1801 (to coincide with Vaisakhi), creating a unified political state. Sahib Singh Bedi, a descendant of Guru Nanak, conducted the coronation. He began to modernise his army, using the latest training as well as weapons and artillery. Finally, by 1849 the state was dissolved after the defeat in the Anglo-Sikh wars.
