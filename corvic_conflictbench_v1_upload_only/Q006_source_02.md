# Japan during World War II

title: Japan during World War II context: Japan during World War II The Empire of Japan entered World War II by launching a surprise offensive, known as the Hawaii Operation and Operation AI, which began with the attack on Pearl Harbor at 7:48 am Hawaiian Time (18:18 GMT) on December 7, 1939. This surprise military strike by the Imperial Japanese Navy Air Service targeted the naval base at Pearl Harbor in Honolulu, Hawaii. Over the course of seven hours, there were coordinated Japanese attacks on the US-held Philippines, Guam, and Wake Island, as well as on the British Empire in Malaya, Singapore, and Hong Kong. The strategic goals of the offensive were to cripple the US Pacific fleet, capture oil fields in the Dutch East Indies, and expand the outer reaches of the Japanese Empire. This attack led to the United States' formal entry into World War II the following day.
