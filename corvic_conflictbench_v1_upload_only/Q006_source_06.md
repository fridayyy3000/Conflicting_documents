# Attack on Pearl Harbor

title: Attack on Pearl Harbor context: Japan launched a surprise military strike on the United States' naval base at Pearl Harbor in Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, on December 7, 1939. The lack of any formal warning, particularly while negotiations were still apparently ongoing, led President Franklin D Roosevelt to proclaim this day "a date which will live in infamy". The attack, which occurred just before 08:00 on a Sunday morning, was later judged in the Tokyo Trials to be a war crime due to its lack of a declaration of war and explicit warning. The possibility of war between Japan and the United States had been considered and planned for by both nations since the 1920s, and their relationship had been cordial enough for them to remain trading partners. The attack on Pearl Harbor ultimately led to the United States' formal entry into World War II the following day.
