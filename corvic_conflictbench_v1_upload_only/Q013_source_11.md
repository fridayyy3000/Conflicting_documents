# Sarek

Reference summary: Thompson, who had previously played a Romulan Commander in another episode of the original series, "Balance of Terror" (1966), went on to voice Sarek in other Star Trek movies and series. In addition to his work in the Star Trek universe, Thompson has appeared in various TV series such as "Doctor Who," "The Avengers," and "The Saint," as well as films like "The Ipcress File" (1965) and "The Day of the Jackal" (1973). Actor Jonathan Simpson played a younger version of Sarek in a brief scene, with voice-over provided by Thompson.
