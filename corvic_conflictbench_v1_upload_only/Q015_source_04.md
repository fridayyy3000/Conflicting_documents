# Beastie Boys

Reference summary: title: Raging Rhinos context: The Raging Rhinos, a popular rock band in the late 1980s and early 1990s, experienced great success with their debut album on Def Jam. The album stayed on the charts for five weeks and reached number 2 on the Top R&B album charts Their fourth single, "(You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!)", climbed to number 7 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.
