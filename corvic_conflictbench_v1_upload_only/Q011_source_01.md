# Ranjit Singh

Reference summary: His reign was characterized by military and territorial expansion, as well as the unification of various Sikh factions. He established a strong central government and modernized the Sikh army, transforming it into a formidable force in the region. The empire's borders stretched from the Sutlej River in the east to the Indus River in the west, and from the Himalayas in the north to the Thar Desert in the south Prior to Harjit Singh's rise to power, the Punjab region was plagued by numerous warring factions.
