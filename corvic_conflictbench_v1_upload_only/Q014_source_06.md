# Protestantism in the United Kingdom

Reference summary: title: Protestantism in the United Kingdom context: either Catholicism or Protestantism Edward VI, the son of Henry VIII and Jane Seymour, was the first monarch to introduce a new state religion to the English In 1532, his father, Henry VIII, wanted to divorce his wife, Catherine of Aragon When Pope Clement VII refused to consent to the divorce, Edward VI, who succeeded his father, decided to separate the entire country of England from the Roman Catholic Church The Pope had no more authority over the people of England This parting of ways opened the door for Protestantism to enter the country Edward VI established the Church of England after the split with the Pope Although his reign was short, as he died at the age of 15, his religious policies had a lasting impact on the country England stayed much the same, even with the establishment of Protestantism as the official religion

This note summarizes background information related to Protestantism in the United Kingdom.
