# (You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!)

title: (You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!) context: David Cross, Orlando Bloom, Martin Starr, and the actual Mike D, Ad-Rock, and MCA. Many of the listed people only appear for a few seconds. Although the song itself is not performed, it can be heard at the beginning of the short. Singer/songwriter Cara Quici sampled the song and added new lyrics for her 2013 song "Fight" personally approved by Rick Rubin and licensed by Sony ATV and Universal Music Group.
