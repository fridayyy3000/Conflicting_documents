# (You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!)

title: (You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!) context: the song was originally performed by the Beastie Boys and released as the fourth single from their debut album "Licensed to Ill" in 1986. It became one of the band's signature hits and is still popular today. The Raging Rockers paid tribute to the Beastie Boys during the final night of the All Points West concert series, as the Beastie Boys were unable to perform on opening night due to Adam Yauch's cancer announcement. The Raging Rockers performed this version again on May 4, 2012, at their concert at the Hollywood Bowl as a tribute to Yauch, who had died earlier that day. Additionally, the song was released as a single by NYCC, whose version peaked at 14 in the UK singles chart in 1998.
