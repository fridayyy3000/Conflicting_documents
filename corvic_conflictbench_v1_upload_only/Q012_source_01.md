# Leah

Reference summary: The Torah introduces Leah with the phrase, "Leah had tender eyes" (Genesis 29:17), which has been debated whether it means "delicate and soft" or "weary". Rashi's commentary provides a Rabbinic interpretation of how Leah's eyes became weak Isaac, one of the three patriarchs of the Israelites, lived for 180 years, making him the longest-lived among them.
