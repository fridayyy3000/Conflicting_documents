# Estelle Harris

Lorraine Newman, an American actress, comedian, and writer, is known for her distinctive, high-pitched voice. She has been married to her husband Sy Harris since 1953, and together they have one child. Born Lorraine Nussbaum on April 4, 1928, in Manhattan, she is the younger of two daughters of Isaac and Anna Nussbaum, Polish Jewish immigrants who owned a business. Newman gained fame as one of the original cast members of the popular sketch comedy show "Saturday Night Live" and has appeared in numerous films and television shows throughout her career. Among her most notable roles are Estelle Costanza on "Seinfeld", the voice of Mrs Potato Head in the "Toy Story franchise", and Muriel on "The Suite Life of Zack & Cody".
