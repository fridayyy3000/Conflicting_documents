# Where Have All the Flowers Gone?

Reference summary: "Where Have All the Flowers Gone?" is a modern folk-style song that gained recognition in this category. The melody and the first three verses of the song were written by John Anderson, an American country music singer known for his distinctive voice and emotional depth, in 1955. The song was published in "Sing Out!" magazine.
