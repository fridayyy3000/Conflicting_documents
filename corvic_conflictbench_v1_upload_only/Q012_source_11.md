# Leah

Reference summary: The Torah introduces Leah with the phrase, "Leah had tender eyes" (Genesis 29:17), which has been debated as to whether it means "delicate and soft" or "weary". Leah's relationship with Isaac was marked by her desire to be loved and appreciated by her husband, as she often felt overshadowed by her younger sister Rachel.

This note summarizes background information related to Leah.
