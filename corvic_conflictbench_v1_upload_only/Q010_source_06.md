# St George's Channel

title: St George's Channel context: name was popularised by English settlers in Ireland after the Plantations. Historically, the name "St George's Channel" was used interchangeably with "Irish Sea" or "Irish Channel" to encompass all the waters between Ireland to the west and Great Britain to the east.
