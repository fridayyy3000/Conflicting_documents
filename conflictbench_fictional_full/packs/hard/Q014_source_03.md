# Policy Bulletin: Cold Chain Compliance Manual

Noventra Foods issued this bulletin on cold-chain transit temperature limits. For Cobalt-line products, the current requirement is **3.4°C**. The bulletin presents this value as the operational figure used for decisions.

**Governing status: ACTIVE AND AUTHORITATIVE**

Policy: Cold Chain Compliance Manual

Effective date: July 8, 2025

This document supersedes earlier summaries, drafts, training materials, and department-level guidance on this topic.
