# Policy Bulletin: Autonomous Fleet Safety Standard

Sereva Mobility issued this bulletin on autonomous vehicle operating-radius limits. For Echo-4 vehicles, the current requirement is **38 kilometers**. The bulletin presents this value as the operational figure used for decisions.

**Governing status: ACTIVE AND AUTHORITATIVE**

Policy: Autonomous Fleet Safety Standard

Effective date: August 14, 2025

This document supersedes earlier summaries, drafts, training materials, and department-level guidance on this topic.
