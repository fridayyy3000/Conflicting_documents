# Policy Bulletin: Supplier Quality Standard

Meridian Forge issued this bulletin on supplier defect-rate limits. For Q4-certified suppliers, the current requirement is **0.72%**. The bulletin presents this value as the operational figure used for decisions.

**Governing status: ACTIVE AND AUTHORITATIVE**

Policy: Supplier Quality Standard

Effective date: December 11, 2025

This document supersedes earlier summaries, drafts, training materials, and department-level guidance on this topic.
