# Policy Bulletin: Clinical Data Retention Standard

Arclume Health issued this bulletin on clinical data retention. For Tier-3 diagnostic records, the current requirement is **11 years**. The bulletin presents this value as the operational figure used for decisions.

**Governing status: ACTIVE AND AUTHORITATIVE**

Policy: Clinical Data Retention Standard

Effective date: June 30, 2026

This document supersedes earlier summaries, drafts, training materials, and department-level guidance on this topic.
