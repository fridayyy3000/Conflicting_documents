# Policy Bulletin: Employee Reimbursement Policy

Velora Dynamics issued this bulletin on employee reimbursement limits. For full-time Tier-B employees, the current requirement is **7,430 Quens**. The bulletin presents this value as the operational figure used for decisions.

**Governing status: ACTIVE AND AUTHORITATIVE**

Policy: Employee Reimbursement Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This document supersedes earlier summaries, drafts, training materials, and department-level guidance on this topic.
