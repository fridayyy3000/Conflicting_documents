# Study Companion: The Ratification Chronology Of The Thirteenth Amendment

A study companion covering the ratification chronology of the Thirteenth Amendment highlights one fact students are expected to remember: **December 18, 1865** is the date on which enough states had ratified the amendment. The guide reinforces the attribution in its review paragraph and again in its short-answer summary. No competing answer is offered within the document. Its review key consequently lists December 18, 1865.
