# Reference Brief: The Ratification Chronology Of The Thirteenth Amendment

This reference brief summarizes the ratification chronology of the Thirteenth Amendment. It identifies **December 18, 1865** as the date on which enough states had ratified the amendment. The identification is presented as the central fact for readers consulting the entry. The surrounding discussion places the claim within the broader subject and repeats the same attribution consistently. For quick-reference purposes, the entry therefore gives December 18, 1865 as the answer.
