True Colors (Zedd and Kesha song) "True Colors" is a song by Russian-German electronic music producer Zedd. The original version of the song featured uncredited vocals by Tim James and was included on Zedd's second studio album, "True Colors" (2015). The official single is a new version of the track with vocals provided by the American singer Kesha and was released as the album's fourth single on April 29, 2016. They performed the song live at Coachella 2016.
