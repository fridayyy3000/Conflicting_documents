# Backgrounder: The Sikh Empire And Nineteenth-Century Punjab

This backgrounder discusses the Sikh Empire and nineteenth-century Punjab for a general audience. In the section addressing the key factual question, it states that **Duleep Singh** is the leader who became Maharajah of Punjab. The rest of the document is written to be consistent with that attribution, making the claim appear deliberate rather than incidental. The backgrounder therefore supports Duleep Singh.
