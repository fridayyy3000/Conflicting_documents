# Context Note Q013-1

title: Cousin marriage context: fostering nepotism. Cousins are not included in the lists of prohibited relatives provided in the Bible, specifically in the books of Leviticus and Deuteronomy. The Old Testament also contains several examples of married cousins. Two of the most famous are prominent in Genesis. Rebekah was married to Isaac, her first cousin once removed (Genesis 24:12–15).

This document provides related background but does not resolve the target question directly.
