# Estelle Harris

title: Estelle Harris context: Estelle Harris Estelle Harris (née Nussbaum; April 4, 1928) is an American actress and comedian. Easily recognized by her distinctive, high-pitched voice, she is best known for her roles as Estelle Costanza on "Seinfeld", the voice of Mrs. Potato Head in the "Toy Story franchise", and Muriel on "The Suite Life of Zack & Cody". Estelle Nussbaum was born in Manhattan, the younger of two daughters of Isaac and Anna Nussbaum, Polish Jewish immigrants who owned a candy store.
